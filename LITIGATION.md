# Litigation, Civil Procedure & Prescription

This document is the thematic specification for Québec civil procedure (*Code de procédure civile*), limitation periods and prior notices (*Code civil du Québec*), court topology, litigation taxonomies, case protocols, and deontological safeguards in Pallas Athena. It complements [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) / [GEMINI.md](GEMINI.md), [ACCOUNTING.md](ACCOUNTING.md), [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md).

Authoritative implementations live in `utils/deadlines.py`, `utils/recours.py`, `utils/recurrence.py`, `utils/taxonomie.py` (upstream reference: [docs/Taxonomie des actions en justice (v1.2).md](docs/Taxonomie%20des%20actions%20en%20justice%20(v1.2).md)), `utils/phases.py`, `utils/kyc.py`, `utils/analyse_taxonomies.py`, `utils/analyse_protection.py`, `utils/analyse_blocs.py`, `utils/template_fields.py`, `models/dossier.py`, `models/partie.py`, `models/protocol.py`, `models/task.py`, `models/note.py`, `models/reference.py`, and `services/protocoles.py`.

## Foundational principles

- **The lawyer decides; machine outputs are proposals.** Taxonomy delays (`utils/taxonomie.py`), document analyses, and connector compliance inscriptions (`utils/kyc.py`) are suggestions or `présumé` until confirmed by the lawyer in the web UI. Automated paths never confirm, overwrite, or lower a confirmed choice.
- **Fail upward on privilege & professional secrecy.** Under art. 9 of the Québec *Charter* and art. 60.4 of the *Code des professions*, inadvertent disclosure is asymmetrical (`utils/analyse_protection.py`): doubt retains the most protective plausible regime, automated paths never downgrade protection, and an absent label means undetermined (`None`), never `PUBLIC`.
- **Pure legal math, single authority.** Domain modules in `utils/` import neither Flask nor `models/`. Never duplicate holiday, prorogation, prescription, value-class, or court-file math in routes, templates, or scripts.
- **Orthogonal classification axes.** Never merge, derive, or overload the substantive action taxonomy (`utils/taxonomie.py`), organizational/billing litigation phases (`utils/phases.py`), document/privilege taxonomies (`utils/analyse_taxonomies.py`), and task/expense categories.

## Two calendars, deadlines & recurring series (`utils/deadlines.py`, `utils/recours.py`, `utils/recurrence.py`)

- **`PROCEDURAL` vs. `CIVIL` calendars:** Both exclude Saturdays and the statutory holidays of art. 61(23) *Loi sur l'interprétation* (Sundays included).
  - `PROCEDURAL` (arts. 82–83 *C.p.c.*, default of `utils/deadlines.py`) **also** treats **26 December and 2 January** as *jours fériés* every year regardless of weekday.
  - `CIVIL` (art. 2879 *C.c.Q.* for prescription, invoice due-date prorogation, and *jours ouvrables*) does **not** exclude 26 December or 2 January.
  - Neither calendar invents Sunday → Monday substitutions for 1 January, 24 June, or 25 December: art. 61(23) *L.i.* provides only **2 July** when 1 July falls on a Sunday. Unknown `regime` values raise `ValueError`.
- **Computation vs. lateness:**
  - `compute_deadline` is pure art. 83 *C.p.c.* calendar arithmetic (`PROCEDURAL`, zero clock access): every day counts, and a raw deadline landing on a non-juridical day shifts further in the `direction` of computation (`"after"` → `next_juridical_day`; `"before"` → `prev_juridical_day`).
  - Lateness (`today_mtl`, `effective_due`, `is_past_due`, `days_until`) reads the **Montréal** calendar day (never `date.today()` or UTC, save `today_utc` for invoice-date bounds alone) against the prorogued deadline on its governing `regime`: due Saturday → actionable Monday → late Tuesday. Stored date-only fields sit at UTC midnight and keep their UTC calendar date (never pass them through `to_mtl`).
- **Last day to file (`last_action_day`):** Evaluates on `PROCEDURAL` even when called for a `CIVIL` prescription alert, because interrupting prescription by judicial application requires filing before expiry (art. 2892 *C.c.Q.*) when court registries sit (closed on 26 December and 2 January).
- **Month/year clamping & recurring series:** Whole-month and whole-year additions (`recours.add_period`) clamp end-of-month dates (`31 Jan + 1 month → 28/29 Feb`; `29 Feb + 1 year → 28 Feb`). Recurring series (`utils/recurrence.py`) anchor occurrence `k` from the original start (`amount * k`, never chained), return pure `date` objects converted individually via `tz.mtl_to_utc` so wall-clock times survive DST switches, and never apply art. 83 *C.p.c.* weekend/holiday prorogation.

## Classification axes: actions vs. litigation phases (`utils/taxonomie.py`, `utils/phases.py`)

- **Substantive action taxonomy (`domaine` → `action`):** Since the 2016 *C.p.c.* unified proceedings under a single *demande introductive d'instance* (art. 141 s. *C.p.c.*), `utils/taxonomie.py` classifies the substantive right (`REC-01`, `CST-*`, …; generated from an approved spec — never edit rows by hand). Each action carries indicative `delai`, `delai_types` (`PE` extinctive prescription, `PA` acquisitive prescription, `D` strict *déchéance*, `DR` relievable *déchéance*, `A` prior notice, `R` reasonable delay, `N` no delay, `I` imprescriptible, `S` follows underlying right, `V` variable/to qualify, `F` retrospective window), `a_valider`, factual `point_depart`, `ref_delai` (source of the delay), `ref_fondement` (seat of the right of action), structured `avis` (`Avis`), and a suggested `prescription_type` (`""` wherever regime-dependent, reasonable, retrospective, acquisitive, or `-99` catch-all).
- **Litigation phases (`phase` / `sous_phase`, Phase O):** `utils/phases.py` is an organizational and billing axis — never a source of deadlines (`ADM` and `HOR` default non-billable). Codes are strict ASCII so DavX5 `CATEGORIES` round-trips cannot corrupt accents (`CTS` *Contestation* is a visual anagram of action domain `CST` *Construction* — user-facing surfaces always display French labels). Only `categorie == "tronc"` is ordered (`ordre` 1–9); calendar-duration analytics may only run over the ordered `tronc`.

## Prescription, prior notices & significations (`utils/recours.py`, `models/dossier.py`)

- **Generic period keys & `compute_date_pour_agir`:** `recours.PRESCRIPTION_PERIODS` uses generic duration labels (`3_ans` → « 3 ans », without hardcoding one article) and covers *déchéance* and *avis* durations alongside prescription. `compute_date_pour_agir` adds the confirmed period to `droit_action_date` and prorogues Saturday/*férié* landings forward on `CIVIL` (art. 2879 al. 2 *C.c.Q.*). In `models/dossier.py`, `imprescriptible` clears `prescription_date`, while `""` or `autre` preserves any hand-entered date.
- **Type-aware orchestration (`compute_echeances`):** Composes existing `CIVIL` arithmetic (`deadlines.add_jours_ouvrables` serves only `AVIS_PERIODS`'s `3_jours_ouvrables`, which never enters the prescription dropdown). Tokens set severity and warnings (`rouge` on strict *déchéance* `D` — neither interruptible nor suspensible in principle; `orange` on relievable *déchéance* `DR`); `PA` yields a defensive adverse-possession maturity; prior notices (`action.avis`, manual `date_avis`) have independent factual starting points, require scenario confirmation when `conditionnel`, and never replace the principal deadline.
- **Events & `dossier.derive_prescription` (three-surface parity):** Raw `prescription_date` is never mutated by subsequent events. Manual `prescription_events` (`interruption_depot` arts. 2892/2896, `interruption_reconnaissance` art. 2898, `suspension` art. 2904 s., `renonciation` art. 2883 s.) plus legacy `prise_action_date` project `{status, date_effective}` via `dossier.derive_prescription`, shared by dashboard alerts, dossier list/detail badges, and MCP `get_dossier` / `get_agenda`:
  - `interruption_depot` returns `status="interrompue"`, `date_effective=None` because interruption continues until judgment (art. 2896 *C.c.Q.*); whether service occurred within 60 days of expiry (art. 2892 al. 1 *C.c.Q.*) is verified by the lawyer, never asserted by code.
  - `interruption_reconnaissance` and `renonciation` restart a confirmed period from the event date (`a_verifier` if no computable period is confirmed); `suspension` shifts the effective date by `end_date - date` and prorogues on `CIVIL`.
- **Service per party (`significations`):** Recorded manually on the dossier per party (`partie_id`, `date`, `mode`, `huissier_id`, `pv_document_id`, `superseded_by`, `confirmee`) because answer and representation deadlines (arts. 145, 147 *C.p.c.*) run individually per party served.

## Forums, court file numbers, parties & value classes (`models/reference.py`, `models/dossier.py`, `utils/template_fields.py`)

- **Four-way `forum_type` & plumitif numbers:** `"judiciaire"` parses `NNN-NN-NNNNNN-NNN` via `reference.parse_court_file_number` into 3-digit `greffe` and 2-digit `juridiction`; `"administratif"` and `"federal"` resolve bodies from `reference._FORUMS` (matching alpha prefixes like `TAL-…` without treating federal courts as administrative tribunals) and keep file numbers verbatim; `"prejudiciaire"` (before filing) stores the district and forces `PREJUDICIAIRE_FILE_NUMBER` (`"Préjudiciaire"`) until a real court number replaces it.
- **Palais vs. greffe:** `_PALAIS` indexes physical buildings (`palais` vs. MJQ `point_de_service`), whereas `_GREFFES` indexes court registries referencing `palais_key` (`None` marks an itinerant circuit registry or unmapped address — distinct from the greffe-level `point_de_service` circuit flag; never guess an address).
- **Value classes (`recours.compute_class`):** Maps `valeur` in integer cents to Roman classes `I`–`IV` (`VALUE_CLASSES` / `TOP_CLASS`), with upper bounds inclusive at the cent (`15 000,00 $` → `I`; `15 000,01 $` → `II`).
- **Litigation roles, counsel & addresses:** Dossier party entries hold per-party `roles ⊆ PARTY_ROLES` (multiple allowed, e.g., `défendeur` + `demandeur reconventionnel`) and counsel links (`client_ids`, `opposing_party_ids`, and `avocat_ids` mirror for `array_contains` queries). Top-level `dossier.role` derives from the first client with a role for gabarit placeholders (`utils/template_fields.py`, where `{{civilité}}` stays passthrough because court pleadings omit it), and `template_fields.selected_address` is the single authority for party address resolution.

## Case protocols, théorie de la cause & compliance (`models/protocol.py`, `services/protocoles.py`, `utils/analyse_blocs.py`, `utils/kyc.py`)

- **One active protocol & tribunal coherence:** At most one `actif` protocol per dossier, enforced fail-closed inside the writing transaction (`models/protocol.py`). `regime_mismatch` guards *C.p.c.* templates against `dossier.tribunal` and `forum_type`: `cq_simplifié` (arts. 535.1 s. *C.p.c.*) requires `Cour du Québec`; `cs_ordinaire` (arts. 145–148, 173 *C.p.c.*) requires `Cour supérieure`; `conventionnel` is the unrestricted template for non-judicial forums, appeals, or custom schedules.
- **Mandatory steps & linked tasks:** Mandatory *C.p.c.* steps lock their legal text (`title`, `description`, `cpc_reference`), forbid erasing `deadline_date`, and lock statutory deadlines (`DEADLINE_LOCKED`, e.g., art. 173(1) *C.p.c.* *mise en état*). Step status changes solely through `set_step_status` (cascading two-way with `models/task.py` and auto-closing/reopening `closed_by="auto"` protocols). Moving a step's deadline realigns its linked open task via `services/protocoles.py` only when the task still sits on the step's prior date (leaving lawyer-customized `diverged` dates untouched) and bumps its DAV CTag.
- **Théorie de la cause (`is_analyse`):** At most one undated `is_analyse` Markdown note (`models/note.py`) per dossier, seeded with `## Bloc A` through `## Bloc H`. Partial bloc edits (`utils/analyse_blocs.py`) require all eight bloc headings present exactly once and refuse (`structure_modifiee`) unless every untouched bloc, header, and interstitial segment remains byte-identical.
- **Client identification & conflict checks (`utils/kyc.py`, `models/partie.py`):** Separates identification (`identifié`, arts. 13–14 of the *Barreau* accounting regulation, owed in every file; successor to retired `exempté`) from document verification (`vérifié`, arts. 20–23, when handling non-exempt funds), alongside `conflict_check` (`non_vérifié`, `vérifié`, `conflit_détecté`). Every status transition must name its `source` (`"juriste"` or `"mcp"`): `"mcp"` transitions remain `présumé` (`is_decided` is `False`) until confirmed in the web UI and are refused on any lawyer-decided or confirmed status.
